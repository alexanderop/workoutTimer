# SmartWOD Timer visual references

These images were downloaded from the public [SmartWOD Timer Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=net.smartwod.timer&hl=de) on 2026-08-08. The listing was last updated on 2026-08-04 when inspected.

They are retained only as internal product and interaction references. They remain the property of their respective publisher and must not be used as production artwork, store media, branding, or marketing material for this app.

## Observed product capabilities

The listing and screenshots directly support these reference capabilities:

- AMRAP, For Time, EMOM, Tabata, and Mix timer choices.
- Large timer display and mode-colored progress.
- Integrated round capture and round splits.
- Workout completion summary and notes.
- Workout log.
- Presets.
- Configurable sounds or voices.
- Background continuation as a stated product behavior.
- Tablet layouts that preserve the same hierarchy at a wider size.

The specification intentionally narrows the first release to the four core modes. Mix, casting, and richer sharing are follow-up work.
